# Privacy Policy

**My Guide** — by BBotnics Industries
Last updated: 10 November 2026

My Guide is an offline Bible app. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: **everything you create stays on your phone. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your information.**

## 1. Information we collect

**None.** My Guide has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. BBotnics Industries does not run any server that the app talks to, and we never receive your notes, highlights, reading history, recordings, photos or any other information.

## 2. Information stored on your phone

The app saves the following **only on your device**, in the app's private storage:

- Your reading position, reading plan, streak, settings and chosen Bible version
- Highlights, bookmarks, verse notes and reading history
- Notes you write, including voice recordings, photos, drawings and transcripts you add to them
- Your name, if you enter one for the greeting
- Bible versions you import from files
- The natural AI voice files, if you choose to download them

This information never leaves your phone unless you choose to share it (see section 5). Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, permanently deletes all of it. We cannot recover it for you, because we never have a copy.

## 3. Permissions the app asks for

| Permission | Why | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Notifications** | To show your daily morning reading reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent over the internet. | When the app first opens |
| **Microphone** | To record voice notes, and to turn speech into text. Speech is converted to text on your phone; the audio is not saved or sent anywhere. | Only when you tap record (🎙) or transcribe (🗣) in a note |
| **Files** | To pick an audio file to transcribe, or a Bible file to import. The app only sees the file you choose. | Only when you choose "Transcribe an audio file" or "Import a Bible file" |
| **Camera** | To take a photo for a note. | Only when you choose "Take photo" |
| **Photos** | To add an existing photo to a note. Android's photo picker only gives the app the photos you select. | Only when you choose "Choose from gallery" |
| **Internet** | To check whether a newer version of the app exists, and to download the optional natural voices and the speech-to-text model. | When the app opens (version check); when you tap to download a voice or model |

You can refuse or withdraw any permission in your phone's settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

## 4. Internet use

The Bibles, devotionals and prayers are built into the app and work fully offline.

When the app opens and your phone is online, it asks **GitHub** (api.github.com) for the number of the latest My Guide release, so it can tell you when an update is available. This request contains no information about you or your use of the app. If you are offline, nothing happens.

The only other time the app connects to the internet is if you choose to **download a natural AI voice** (about 20 MB each) or **the speech-to-text model** (about 55 MB). The app then contacts **GitHub** (github.com and api.github.com) to download the voice models published by the sherpa-onnx project. Like any website, GitHub receives standard technical information such as your IP address when the download happens; this is covered by [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). No personal information from the app is sent.

## 5. Sharing

When you tap **Share** on a verse or a note, or **Share error report** on the error screen, the app hands the text to your phone's share menu and you choose where it goes (for example WhatsApp or email). The app does not send anything by itself. What happens next is governed by the app you share to.

## 6. Reading aloud

With the **phone voice**, text is spoken by your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine (for example Google Speech Services). That engine is provided by your phone's maker and is covered by their privacy terms. With the **natural AI voice**, speech is generated entirely on your phone.

## 7. Children

My Guide is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

## 8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. You control all of your data on your own device. This policy is written to meet the requirements of South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and similar laws elsewhere.
